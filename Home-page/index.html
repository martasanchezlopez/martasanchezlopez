About me

Hola! ✨

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the CNRS-IKER (UMR5478) research group in Bayonne, France. My research focuses on the mechanisms involved in sentence processing and production, particularly at the syntax-semantics interface.

I obtained my PhD in Linguistics at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), under the supervision of Dr. Mikel Santesteban and Prof. Itziar Laka. Furthermore, I conducted my doctoral research within the Gogo Elebiduna research group, where I continue to collaborate as an external member.

During my PhD, I investigated the processing correlates of the experiencer role in transitive and intransitive sentences in Spanish. This work sparked my interest in event roles, that I continue to experience and explore in my postdoctoral research. You can read more about my findings and research in my doctoral dissertation.

Beyond event roles, I am also interested in eye-tracking and EEG methodologies, as well as Bayesian statistics. As you can see in Courses section, I have taken several courses to learn more about these areas, and I am keen to keep learning!

Feel free to get in touch if you would like to know more about my research or explore ways we could work together. I am always excited to learn new things!
